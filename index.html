What is HSS USA?

Hindu Swayamsevak Sangh USA is a 501(c)(3) voluntary, non-profit, social, educational, and cultural organization founded in 1989.

Community Organization

We coordinate the diverse Hindu American community across 220+ branches nationwide to address collective challenges and contribute to society.

Values-Based Education

We conduct regular educational programs for children, youth, and adults, promoting discipline, self-confidence, teamwork, and selfless service.

Civic Engagement

Through service activities and community outreach, we foster civic duty, responsibility, and volunteerism among our members.

Our Vision

Our Mission

To organize a diverse and vibrant Hindu American society that can collectively address its challenges, and contribute to the welfare of the world.

What We Do

Our work focuses on education, service, and community building to strengthen American society.

📚

Educational Programs

Values-based education through Balagokulam (children's programs), youth development, and adult learning initiatives that promote character building and leadership.

🤲

Community Service

Sewa (selfless service) activities including disaster relief, food drives, blood donations, and support for vulnerable communities during crises like COVID-19.

🌉

Civic Engagement

Building bridges with local governments, interfaith organizations, and community groups to promote understanding and collaborative problem-solving.

🎭

Cultural Preservation

Celebrating and sharing Hindu heritage through festivals, cultural events, and educational outreach that enriches American multicultural society.

👥

Youth Development

Leadership training programs for young Americans that develop confidence, teamwork skills, and commitment to service.

💬

Interfaith Dialogue

Active participation in interfaith activities and collaboration with other faith-based organizations on projects of common interest.

Our Impact

Recent examples of HSS USA's service to American communities

LA Wildfire Relief

HSS volunteers provided immediate assistance and relief services during the Los Angeles wildfires, supporting affected communities.

COVID-19 Response

Over 2,000 volunteers across 27 states mobilized to provide masks for first responders, food for hospital staff, and support for vulnerable populations.

Education Support

Organized school supply donation drives nationwide to support students and teachers in underserved communities.

Cultural Bridge Building

Celebrated Raksha Bandhan as Universal Oneness Day, promoting unity and understanding across different communities.

Black History Month

HSS chapters participated in Black History Month celebrations nationwide, demonstrating commitment to multicultural unity.

Teacher Appreciation

Guru Vandana (Teacher Appreciation) events across the country honor educators and promote the value of learning in society.

Our Core Values

Non-Political

HSS USA does not participate in political activism or support any political ideology.

Inclusive

Everyone who agrees with our mission and vision is welcome to join our activities.

Volunteer-Driven

All activities are managed by dedicated volunteers with no membership fees required.

Service-Oriented

Promoting selfless service (Sewa) to benefit all of American society.
