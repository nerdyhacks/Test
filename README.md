# HSS USA Website Design

A minimal, professional website design for Hindu Swayamsevak Sangh USA (HSS USA) targeting civic leaders and community leaders in the United States.

## Overview

This website design effectively communicates HSS USA's work, vision, and mission to civic and community leaders through a clean, minimal interface that avoids information overload.

## Key Features

### 🎯 Target Audience
- Civic leaders and community leaders in the USA
- Government officials and policymakers
- Community organizations seeking partnerships
- Interfaith dialogue participants

### 📱 Design Principles
- **Minimal Design**: Clean, uncluttered layout focusing on essential information
- **Professional Aesthetic**: Suitable for civic and government audiences
- **Clear Messaging**: Direct communication about HSS USA's purpose and impact
- **Mobile Responsive**: Works seamlessly across all devices

### 🏗️ Website Structure

1. **Hero Section**: Clear value proposition and call-to-action
2. **About HSS USA**: What the organization is and does
3. **Vision & Mission**: Core organizational principles
4. **What We Do**: Six key areas of work with specific examples
5. **Impact**: Recent examples of community service
6. **Core Values**: Key organizational principles
7. **Contact**: How to connect and partner

## Content Highlights

### What is HSS USA?
- 501(c)(3) non-profit organization founded in 1989
- 220+ branches across 50 states
- 100% volunteer-based organization
- Focus on education, service, and civic engagement

### Vision & Mission
- **Vision**: World Peace through Dharma
- **Mission**: Organize diverse Hindu American society to address challenges and contribute to world welfare

### Core Activities
1. **Educational Programs**: Values-based education for all ages
2. **Community Service**: Disaster relief, food drives, community support
3. **Civic Engagement**: Building bridges with government and community groups
4. **Cultural Preservation**: Sharing Hindu heritage to enrich American society
5. **Youth Development**: Leadership training for young Americans
6. **Interfaith Dialogue**: Collaboration with other faith-based organizations

### Recent Impact Examples
- LA Wildfire Relief efforts
- COVID-19 response (2,000+ volunteers across 27 states)
- School supply donation drives
- Black History Month celebrations
- Teacher appreciation events

## Color Scheme

- **Primary Orange**: #ff6b35 (HSS brand color)
- **Secondary Orange**: #f7931e (gradient accent)
- **Text Dark**: #333 (headings and primary text)
- **Text Light**: #666 (body text)
- **Background**: #fff (clean white background)
- **Section Alt**: #f8f9fa (light gray for alternating sections)

## Typography

- **Font Family**: Inter (clean, professional web font)
- **Heading Weights**: 600-700 (strong, authoritative)
- **Body Weight**: 400 (readable, accessible)
- **Responsive Scaling**: Fluid typography for all screen sizes

## Key Messages for Civic Leaders

4. **Community-Focused**: Works to strengthen American society

## Getting Started

1. Open `index.html` in a web browser
2. Or serve locally: `python3 -m http.server 8000`
3. Navigate to `http://localhost:8000`
